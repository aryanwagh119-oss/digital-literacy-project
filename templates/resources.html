Digital Literacy Learning Resources

📱 Smartphone Basics

Learn how to install apps, update software, manage settings and use a browser safely.

🔐 Cyber Safety

Use strong passwords. Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords. Be careful with unexpected links and messages.

💳 UPI & Digital Payments

Verify the recipient before paying and use only official banking/payment applications.

🏛️ Digital Government Services

Use official government websites and verify web addresses before entering personal information.

📰 Information Literacy

Check the source, date and supporting evidence before believing or forwarding online information.

🧑‍💻 Online Forms

Read forms carefully, use official websites, and keep confirmation details when submitting applications.
